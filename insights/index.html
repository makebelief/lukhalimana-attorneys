CRIMINAL LAW · GUIDE

What to Prepare Before a Criminal-Law Consultation

Write down the dates, people and events involved as accurately as possible. Keep any summonses, charge sheets, bail documents or correspondence you have received.

If the matter is urgent or you are in custody, seek immediate assistance from an appropriately qualified legal practitioner.

An attorney can discuss your rights and the procedures that may apply to your specific circumstances.

RAF CLAIMS · GUIDE

Understanding a Road Accident Claim

Accident details, medical records, police records and information about involved vehicles may be relevant to an initial consultation.

The claims process includes legal requirements and time limits that vary by circumstance. A practitioner can help assess which rules apply.

Avoid relying on general online guides for deadlines or eligibility in a particular claim.

ESTATES · GUIDE

Starting Deceased Estate Administration

Administration may involve reporting a death, identifying the executor and gathering identification, financial and estate documents.

The steps depend on the nature of the estate and the applicable legal requirements.

Legal assistance can help a family understand its responsibilities and the next administrative steps.
